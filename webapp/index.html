Load the model (Settings), collect or import bookmarks, then search.

  1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar: 📌 xbm collect
  2. Click Open x.com bookmarks below — a new tab opens.
  3. In that tab, click the 📌 xbm collect bookmark. It pages through your bookmarks and sends them back here automatically.

Notes: the collector runs inside the x.com tab (same-origin, your cookies stay there). X does not expose bookmark timestamps, so dates fall back to tweet time. Delete-mirroring requires a full re-collect.
